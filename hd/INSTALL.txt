Lords of the Realm II HD 1.6.0: an unofficial fan patch for the GOG and Steam versions of the game.
You need your own copy of the game.

INSTALL
Run Lords2-HD-1.6.0-setup.exe with the game closed: it finds the GOG or Steam game (or asks for the
folder holding Lords2.exe). From the zip instead: unzip everything into the game's folder, so that
ddraw.dll, dplayx.dll, lords2hd.ini and lords2hd.pak sit next to Lords2.exe. Then start the game as usual.

UNINSTALL
From Windows' Apps list; or delete ddraw.dll, dplayx.dll, lords2hd.ini, lords2hd.pak, lords2hd.log
and the lords2hd folder. Your game files are not changed.

MULTIPLAYER
- dplayx.dll is the LotR2 Fix multiplayer layer by Wolfe and Socius (see lords2hd\MULTIPLAYER-CREDITS.md):
  it replaces Windows' DirectPlay with its own network code over TCP port 47700, checks every turn
  that all players' games agree and repairs them if not, and fixes the game's own multiplayer
  desyncs. Every player needs the same dplayx.dll (this patch's, or the same build from the Discord).
- Multiplayer offers four ways in: Host an internet game, Host a private game, Join an internet game
  and Join by address.
- INTERNET GAMES are listed on the mod's lobby server: no address and no port forward are needed, and
  nobody sees anyone's IP address. Anyone can join a listed game. relay_host=0 in lords2hd.ini
  [Multiplayer] removes "Host an internet game".
- PRIVATE GAMES / BY ADDRESS: the host allows the Firewall prompt; the other players need to reach the
  host's TCP port 47700 (same network, a port forward, or a VPN such as ZeroTier, Tailscale or Radmin)
  and type the host's address. GameRanger works as before.
- The patch's campaign fixes (food ration, wheat, the computer's castles, besieged castles) run in a
  multiplayer game when the host has them on and every player has this same version of the patch;
  otherwise everyone plays the original rules. The battle fixes and Enemy tactics stay off in
  multiplayer. The game keeps running when you switch windows, so nobody waits for you.
- MULTIPLAYER LOGS: each multiplayer game writes a folder lords2hd-mp\<date and time>\ with what is
  needed to debug the session (versions, settings, all the logs and a summary of the session; the
  logs of all players share one clock, so they line up). If a game desyncs or crashes, every player zips their folder for that game and posts it
  on the community Discord (https://discord.gg/uBzaMGjUR). The ten newest games are kept.

GAME FIXES (single player; lords2hd.ini [Game]; the battle ones are on F10's Battles tab)
- Mop-Up fix (MopUpFix): your troops keep fighting after their first kill instead of standing idle
- Improved pathfinding (JitterFix): no stepping back and forth, no swapping places over and over
- Group cohesion (GroupMoveFix): groups keep together round obstacles, and yours stay where you left
  them instead of re-forming every few seconds (a selection still brings them together)
- Improved engagement (EngageFix): idle soldiers fight enemies close by (never baited far away, and
  only when they aren't outnumbered there; next to stakes, water, a castle wall, the gate or the moat they hold the line); archers back
  up their soldiers' fights, don't chase after a fight, step back from a charge (not behind stakes)
  and out of melee
- The computer no longer starts a castle in every county at once (the Bishop's royal castles)
- A wheat field lost after planting costs its grain once, not twice
- No army can enter or strengthen a castle under siege (the game's own rule)
ENEMY TACTICS (BattleAI, F10 Battles; not a fix but a choice): Normal (the game's own, default), Hard
(the computer keeps to attack or defence, keeps its archers behind its soldiers, charges together and
storms a bridge you hold together), Legendary (Hard plus picked targets, regrouping and unit counters
for both sides). See the README on GitHub for the full rules.

WHAT IT DOES
- Runs fullscreen at your monitor's resolution, without switching its display mode
- Fills widescreen monitors on the county map, the kingdom overview and in battles; battles show a
  much larger field
- Full-screen paintings (merchant, armoury, castle builder, menus) widened to fill the screen
- Double-detail paintings, ground, buildings, troops, minimap, panels and lettering
- Foam rolling in along the coast on the county map
- Message scrolls, yes/no questions and the end-of-battle scroll centred over the map or battlefield
- A smaller mouse cursor
- Leaves the CPU mostly idle (the game runs at 120 frames a second, its own speed unchanged)
- The dialogue and unit voices restored (Lords2Resounded, see NOTICE.md)
- An army scroll while one of your armies is selected; WASD scrolling; , and . jump between your
  counties and armies; fresh diplomatic letters

VIDEOS AND MUSIC
- Videos: the disc-quality videos are included (in lords2hd.pak) and play instead of the GOG ones,
  with upscaled frames drawn over them (HDVideos=0 turns those off).
- Music (optional, not included): put the MP3s of the composer's 2020 remastered soundtrack, with their release names
  (for example "07. Scroll 1 (1996, Remastered).mp3"), in lords2hd\music. At the next start the
  patch converts them and cuts each as Lords2Resounded edits the soundtrack, so they loop
  seamlessly; the game then plays them instead of its own tracks. Or copy the WAVs made by the
  Lords2Resounded tool (github.com/gqzomer/Lords2Resounded) into lords2hd\music.

SETTINGS
Press F10 in the game for the settings panel (four tabs: Display, Performance, Quality of life and
Battles; Tab switches them; a right-click closes it): most changes apply at once and are saved, the
battle fixes from the next battle. F8 is the
game's own key again. Ctrl+F10 switches the FPS counter. Or edit lords2hd.ini:
Enabled=0       the stock game
Fullscreen=0    a window; WindowScale sets its size, or WindowHeight its height in pixels
Wide=0          4:3 instead of widescreen
BattleZoom=0    battles at normal size
WheelZoom=0     the mouse wheel no longer zooms the battlefield in and out (2: up to 4x instead of 2x)
CountyZoom=0    the mouse wheel no longer zooms the county map out (twice as wide and tall) and back
MinimapPop=1    outlined troops on the battle minimap (easier to see, black especially)
Seasons=0       the original Town Center and peasants all year (on: it follows the season, with new peasants, from the next visit)
JitterFix=0, GroupMoveFix=0, EngageFix=0, MopUpFix=0    each battle fix off (F10, Battles)
HDScreens=0, HDTiles=0, SharpText=0, ShoreWaves=0    turn each kind of HD art off
HDVideos=0      the videos at their original detail
Upscale=fsr     AMD FSR 1 (default); catmullrom = the scaling of 1.3, bilinear = softer still
Renderer=cpu    the processor draws the picture instead of the graphics card (after a restart)
RepeatFrames=0  a refresh with nothing new from the game shows nothing new (the counter then dips)
FpsCounter=1    frames a second in the corner; 2 = detailed timings
CursorScale=50  cursor size in percent
FrameLimit=120  game frames a second; 0 = as fast as the CPU allows, as the original
TutorialOnce=0  show the tutorial scrolls every time, as the original (default: once each)
WASD=0          W, A, S, D do not scroll the map
ArrowKeys=0     the arrow keys do not scroll the map
BattleAI=1      Hard enemy tactics in field battles; 2 = Legendary; 0 = Normal, the game's own (default)
CycleKeys=0     , and . (and the < > buttons) do not jump through your counties and armies
ArmyPanel=0     no army scroll beside the side panel while one of your armies is selected
Letters=0       diplomatic letters start empty, as the original (default: a fresh letter each time)
Resounded=0     the game's own voices instead of the ones restored by Lords2Resounded

ABOUT THE ART
The HD art and the videos' frames are the game's own, enlarged with an AI upscaler. Some sides of the
widened paintings are built from the painting's own pixels, the others were painted by an AI image
model. The seasonal Town Center (AI-made) and its new hand-drawn peasants are community art by
AllYourBases and CaptainRoberts; see NOTICE.md.

REQUIREMENTS
The GOG or Steam version of the game, Windows, and a screen of 720p (1280x720) or larger. GPU drawing
needs Direct3D 11 (feature level 11.0) graphics from any maker: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 400 or newer, AMD
Radeon HD 5000 or newer, Intel HD Graphics 4000 or newer. AMD FSR 1 is not limited to AMD cards. On
older graphics the patch draws on the processor by itself, and FSR still works there.

BUGS
After a crash, keep lords2hd.log from the game's folder straight away: it is rewritten at each start.
For multiplayer problems, every player sends their lords2hd-mp folder of that game (see MULTIPLAYER).
Report on the Discord (https://discord.gg/uBzaMGjUR) or at github.com/skaldic/lords2-hd/issues.
Say which monitor you use and whether another ddraw.dll wrapper or an overlay is running.
