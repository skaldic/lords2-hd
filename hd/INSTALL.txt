Lords of the Realm II HD 1.1.1: an unofficial fan patch for the GOG version of the game.
You need your own copy of the game.

INSTALL
Unzip everything into the game's folder, the one holding Lords2.exe, so that ddraw.dll and
lords2hd.ini sit next to it. Then start the game as usual.

UNINSTALL
Delete ddraw.dll, lords2hd.ini, lords2hd.log and the lords2hd folder. Your game files are not changed.

WHAT IT DOES
- Runs fullscreen at your monitor's resolution, without switching its display mode
- Fills widescreen monitors on the county map, the kingdom overview and in battles; battles show a
  much larger field
- Full-screen paintings (merchant, armoury, castle builder, menus) widened to fill the screen
- Double-detail paintings, ground, buildings, troops, minimap, panels and lettering
- Foam rolling in along the coast on the county map
- Message scrolls, yes/no questions and the end-of-battle scroll centred over the map or battlefield
- A smaller mouse cursor
- Leaves the CPU mostly idle (the game runs at 120 frames a second, its own speed unchanged)

BETTER VIDEOS AND MUSIC (optional, not included)
- Videos: copy the SMK_HIGH videos (GOG forum, Lords of the Realm II SMK_HIGH patch thread) over
  the ones in the game's videos folder. The larger event clips then fit their frame.
- Music: put the MP3s of the composer's 2020 remastered soundtrack, with their release names
  (for example "07. Scroll 1 (1996, Remastered).mp3"), in lords2hd\music. The patch converts them
  at the next start and the game plays them instead of its own tracks.

SETTINGS
Press F8 in the game for the settings panel: most changes apply at once and are saved.
Or edit lords2hd.ini:
Enabled=0       the stock game
Fullscreen=0    a window; WindowScale sets its size, or WindowHeight its height in pixels
Wide=0          4:3 instead of widescreen
BattleZoom=0    battles at normal size
HDScreens=0, HDTiles=0, SharpText=0, ShoreWaves=0    turn each kind of HD art off
Upscale=bilinear  the softer scaling of earlier versions (default: catmullrom, sharper)
CursorScale=50  cursor size in percent
FrameLimit=120  game frames a second; 0 = as fast as the CPU allows, as the original
TutorialOnce=0  show the tutorial scrolls every time, as the original (default: once each)

ABOUT THE ART
The new sides of the widened paintings were painted by an AI image model; see NOTICE.md.

REQUIREMENTS
The GOG version of the game, Windows, and a screen of 720p (1280x720) or larger.

BUGS
After a crash, keep lords2hd.log from the game's folder straight away: it is rewritten at each start.
Say which monitor you use and whether another ddraw.dll wrapper or an overlay is running.
